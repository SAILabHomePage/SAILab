Hyesong Choi is an Assistant Professor in the School of Electronic Engineering at Soongsil University, where she directs the Scalable AI Lab (SAIL). She received her Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Ewha Womans University in August 2025 (Outstanding Dissertation Award, advisor: Prof. Dongbo Min) and joined Soongsil University as full-time faculty the following month. She was a research intern at NAVER AI Lab, a visiting researcher at the University of British Columbia (host: Prof. Kwang Moo Yi), and a visiting scholar at UIUC (host: Prof. James M. Rehg). Her research on efficient and trustworthy multimodal foundation models has appeared in 12 papers at CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, and NeurIPS, and is supported by the NRF Outstanding Early-Career Researcher Program.
